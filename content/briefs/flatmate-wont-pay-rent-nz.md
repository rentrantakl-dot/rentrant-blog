You can't take a flatmate to the Tenancy Tribunal in New Zealand. [Tenancy Services](https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/starting-a-tenancy/flatting/) is explicit: the Residential Tenancies Act 1986 "only covers tenants and landlords, not flatmates."

The right venue is the [Disputes Tribunal](https://www.disputestribunal.govt.nz/). That one distinction is the most useful thing on this page — filing in the wrong place costs you the fee and the weeks it takes to find out.

---

## Why Tenancy Law Has No Answer for You

Anyone who didn't sign the tenancy agreement isn't a tenant. They're a flatmate, and their rights "depend on the agreement they have with the tenant." There's no bond process, no 14-day notice to remedy, none of the machinery that exists between tenants and landlords.

Tenancy Services draws the line directly: *"Only landlord-tenant disputes are heard by the Tenancy Tribunal. If there are any issues between tenants or flatmates, you can go to the Disputes Tribunal."*

---

## The Part That Catches People

If you signed the agreement, you're liable for **all** of the rent — not your share. Tenancy Services: *"Tenants are jointly responsible for all the rent and any damage, not just their own share."*

| Relationship | Governed by | Who's liable |
|---|---|---|
| You → Landlord | Residential Tenancies Act 1986 | You, for the **full** rent |
| Flatmate → You | Your private agreement | Them, for their share |

> **Warning:** Don't withhold rent from the landlord to force the issue. You're the one on the agreement, so the notices land on you. Keep the landlord paid in full and pursue the flatmate separately. It's galling, and it's the only approach that doesn't make your position worse.

---

## What Recovery Actually Costs

The Disputes Tribunal's limit rose from $30,000 to **$60,000 on 24 January 2026**, so a flatmate rent debt sits comfortably inside it. [Filing fees](https://www.disputestribunal.govt.nz/forms-and-fees/) scale with the claim:

| Claim amount | Fee |
|---|---|
| Less than $2,000 | $62 |
| $2,000 to less than $5,000 | $124 |
| $5,000 to $30,000 | $248 |

Fees are non-refundable, though the Referee may order the other party to pay yours back.

Before you file, send a **written demand** — a calm, dated message asking for a specific sum by a specific date. Sometimes it works. When it doesn't, it's evidence you tried. Bring bank records, the rent ledger, and any messages where the arrangement was discussed.

Next flat, fill in the free [flat/house sharing agreement template](https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/starting-a-tenancy/flatting/). Twenty minutes then is the difference between "here's what we agreed" and "here's what I say we agreed."
